Robot Learning, Foundation-Model Deployment, and Data Curation — Galbot

Jan 2026 – Jun 2026 · Embodied Intelligence Algorithm Department · Research group led by He Wang, Assistant Professor, Peking University

  • Surveyed depth-aware VLA policies and selected DP3 in response to StereoVLA reviewer feedback. Integrated a Dockerized ROS–Franka inference stack with ZED sensing and ZeroMQ communication for StereoVLA baseline adaptation and deployment support.
  • Developed a parallel TFDS-to-LeRobot conversion pipeline for 5 million input SynGrasp-1B trajectories, achieving a 99.5% trajectory conversion success rate and reducing storage for the same dataset from 20 TB to 5 TB (75%).
  • Restored the robot motion-control system for real-world foundation-model deployment; analyzed initial poses in the simulation dataset to guide real-robot grasping experiments, achieving 10/10 successful grasps from selected starting configurations.
  • Reviewed robot-learning data curation methods and attempted to reproduce CUPID-based data selection for clothes folding, examining limitations related to task complexity and dataset suitability.
