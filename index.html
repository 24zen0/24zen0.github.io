I’m a senior undergraduate in Automation Engineering at the Southern University of Science and Technology (SUSTech), advised by Prof. Wei Zhang. Ranked 2nd out of 35 students.

Currently a research intern with the Smart Autonomous Robotics (STAR) Group, advised by Prof. Boyu Zhou. Previously, I was mentored by Prof. He Wang during a research internship at Galbot.

My research interests lie in agent and robot learning, with a focus on enabling learning-based robots to operate reliably in the real world. I am particularly interested in outdoor embodied intelligence for emergency-response and rescue settings, as well as how cognitive principles can inform perception, reasoning, and decision-making in embodied agents.
